# InSmartVest — AI-Powered Investment Platform

**Live app:** https://in-smart-vest.vercel.app

## The Problem

Most people who want to start investing don't know where to begin. They don't have a financially savvy friend, a family member in finance, or money to pay for a financial advisor. They search online and get overwhelmed. They download an app and don't know what to buy. They give up.

This is especially true for students, immigrants, and young professionals who are navigating a new financial system for the first time.


## What InSmartVest Does

InSmartVest is a web application that acts as your personal AI investment advisor — available 24/7, completely free, and built for people who are just getting started.

At the heart of the app is **Afrifa**, an AI advisor powered by Google Gemini. Afrifa learns about you during onboarding — your risk tolerance, how much you want to invest, and whether you are new to investing or already have experience — and then gives you personalized, actionable investment suggestions every time you use the app.


## Who It Is For

- Students and young professionals who want to start investing but don't know how
- Immigrants navigating a new financial system
- Anyone without access to a financial advisor or mentor
- Investors across Africa, Europe, the Americas and beyond



## What You Can Do With InSmartVest

**Get a personalized investment plan**
Tell Afrifa how much you want to invest and your risk level. Afrifa splits your budget between individual stocks and long-term index funds, explains why each allocation makes sense for you, and tells you exactly where to buy them — whether you are in Ghana, Nigeria, the UK or the US.

**Track your investments in real time**
Add any stock or cryptocurrency you own and InSmartVest fetches the live price automatically. You can see your current portfolio value, how much profit or loss you have made since you bought, and a diversification grade that tells you how balanced your portfolio is.

**Set smart market alerts**
Choose any asset and set a percentage drop threshold. The moment that asset falls below your threshold, InSmartVest sends you an email and SMS notification, shows you whether the move is bullish or bearish, rates the significance of the drop, and gives you Afrifa's instant buy/hold/wait recommendation.

**Learn how to invest**
A curated library of YouTube videos filtered by your level, topic and goal — from complete beginners learning what a stock is, to experienced investors exploring index funds and crypto strategies.

**Discover investment apps by country**
A directory of 30+ investment platforms across Africa, Europe, Asia and the Americas — each with Afrifa's rating, a description of who it is best for, and a step-by-step guide on how to open an account in your country.



## The Technology Behind It

InSmartVest is a full-stack web application. Here is what powers it in plain terms:

- **The interface** is built with React and TypeScript — the same technology used by Facebook, Airbnb and many major tech companies
- **The database and login system** runs on Supabase — users can sign in with Google or email, and all data is private and secure
- **The AI advisor** uses Google Gemini — one of the most advanced AI models available — to generate personalized investment suggestions and market analysis
- **Live stock prices** come from Alpha Vantage, a financial data provider used by thousands of developers worldwide
- **Live crypto prices** come from CoinGecko, the world's largest independent cryptocurrency data aggregator
- **Email notifications** are sent through Resend when a price alert fires
- **SMS notifications** are sent through Twilio so users get notified on their phone
- **The app is deployed** on Vercel — every code update automatically goes live within minutes
